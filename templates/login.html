UBC Extended Learning

Never miss a course start date

Course Reminders sends automatic email reminders before UBC Extended Learning courses start. Pick the courses you care about, choose who should be reminded and when, and the emails go out on their own from chhavi.nayyar@ubc.ca.

How it works

  1. 1
    Sign in with your UBC email. We email you a one-time code. No password to remember.
  2. 2
    Pick your courses. Search the Extended Learning catalogue and tick the courses you want.
  3. 3
    Reminders go out automatically. By default 14, 7, 2 and 1 days before each session starts. New sessions and date changes are picked up daily.
Need help? Contact Chhavi Nayyar at chhavi.nayyar@ubc.ca.
